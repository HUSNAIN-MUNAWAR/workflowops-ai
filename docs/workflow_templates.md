# Workflow Templates

Included templates:

- Invoice Approval DAG
- Email-to-Task DAG
- RFP Proposal DAG
- Compliance Evidence DAG
- Support Escalation DAG
- Reliability Tester DAG

Templates include nodes, edges, retry/timeout metadata, approval gates, risk scoring, and report generation. The workflow builder can clone, edit, validate, publish/unpublish, and export/import JSON/YAML-like definitions.
