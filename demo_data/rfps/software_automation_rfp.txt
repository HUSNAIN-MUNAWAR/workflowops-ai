RFP: Governed Workflow Automation Platform
Deadline: 2026-09-01
The vendor must provide a secure workflow automation platform.
The solution shall include human approval before external emails and payment exports.
Mandatory certificate: ISO 27001 certificate or equivalent security policy evidence.
The proposal must include implementation plan, support SLA, and data residency approach.
Risk clause: unlimited liability for data loss.
Risk clause: liquidated damages for late delivery.
